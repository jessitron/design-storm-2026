How this relates to the current demo

The demo offers a choice at Strontia Springs: toward Foothills, or through Waterton Canyon and Conduit 20 toward Marston. Its only starting point is the Hoosier Pass catchment; the glossary’s broader starting-Node rules still need reconciliation.

The existing model ends a Journey when a selected Leg is closed. The demo demonstrates this with a synthetic test fixture, not evidence of a historical closure. Closure policy remains a topic for the architecture session.

History → fiction. Keep swimming.

Agreed direction
Reading rules & open questions

Readings describe each place independently. Historical records may be provisional or revised. Beyond the Episode, fictional variation stays explicitly labeled “Fabricated — not a prediction.”

Open: anchor selection, missing anchors, and gaps inside the Episode. Fabrication algorithms and replay randomness are deferred.

Who owns what?

Proposal · not yet agreed
State ownership and presentation

Journey is a candidate aggregate: its current Node, date, selected Leg, and terminal status must stay consistent. The whole graph and historical dataset do not belong inside that aggregate.

The interface sends visitor actions and displays accepted results. It owns camera and animation state. No decision has been made to use microservices, event sourcing, or a particular persistence technology.

Vocabulary in plain language
Collection System
The real places and physical connections the Journey explores.
Node / Leg
A place you can visit / a physical connection you can traverse.
Trace
A Leg’s known coordinate path. An Unspecified Trace does not mean the Leg is invented. Bore is a retired term.
Journey
One fish’s educational exploration through the Collection System.
Episode / Regime
A named historical time window / a category of water behavior associated with it. Illustrative Journey rules use that context.
Reading
One parameter’s number at one Node for one Journey date.
Provenance
How that particular reading was produced, including whether it is fabricated.
Excursion
A reading crossing a threshold. Threshold authority and the meaning of excursions on fictional values remain unresolved.
