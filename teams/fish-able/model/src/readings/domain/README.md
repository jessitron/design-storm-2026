# Readings domain

Reserved for Reading types, invariants, Provenance and policies that determine
Readings from available observations. Reading decisions belong here, including
historical selection and fallback policies once their open questions are resolved.

Provider formats and fetching stay outside domain models. This placement does not
settle reading-anchor, fabrication, replay or SNOTEL questions, or introduce new
behavior. See the [model structure](../../../README.md#internal-structure).
