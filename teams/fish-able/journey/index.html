    About the Journey · sources & assumptions

    Episodes span May 25–June 20 in 2023 and 2024, when Hoosier Pass SWE declines in the supplied records. “Snow-flush” is an educational interpretation, not an independently validated Regime classification. The configured multiplier is 0.5, rounded up to whole days. No historical closures are asserted.

    Readings use only the exact Journey date. No neighboring dates, invented values or sketch thresholds are substituted. Excursion evaluation is disabled pending validated operational thresholds. Unavailable Readings have no numeric value or Provenance classification.

    Solid Traces reuse committed USGS NLDI and partial OpenStreetMap geometry. Dashed joins connect infrastructure coordinates to nearby Trace vertices; they are schematic. © OpenStreetMap contributors. The demo is a subset of the Collection System, not an inventory of every diversion or reservoir.
